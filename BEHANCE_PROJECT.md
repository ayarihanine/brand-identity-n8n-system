## Project description

Freshy Matcha is a ceremonial matcha concept built around a simple idea: **wellness begins with intention**. The brand treats matcha as more than a drink; it becomes a mindful ritual for slowing down, recentering, and showing up for what matters.

This project brings together the visual identity, packaging language, brand applications, campaign direction, commercial film, and an n8n-powered production system. The result is a calm but distinctive brand world: editorial typography, forest greens, warm mineral neutrals, tactile materials, and a visual rhythm inspired by nature.

### 02 — The idea behind the brand

Freshy Matcha was developed around the tension between modern overstimulation and the quiet ritual of preparing matcha. The identity needed to feel natural without becoming rustic, premium without becoming distant, and expressive without losing clarity.

The creative response is a system that feels **fresh in taste, pure in nature, and made for balance**.

### 04 — The mark and the visual signature

![Freshy Matcha logo](brand/FreshyMatcha-logo.png)

**Caption:** The Freshy Matcha wordmark combines an elegant serif voice with a leaf detail that makes the natural promise unmistakable.

The logo is intentionally simple: a refined lowercase wordmark, a botanical gesture, and a clear supporting descriptor. It gives the brand enough personality to live on packaging while remaining legible across smaller applications.

### 05 — Packaging and premium contrast

![Freshy Matcha dark applications](brand/brand2.png)

**Caption:** The darker application direction creates a richer, more premium expression while retaining the warmth of the natural product world.

Deep green packaging, cream typography, gold-green botanical accents, ceramic surfaces, and bamboo tools work together to make the ritual feel considered from first glance to first sip.

### 06 — Color language

![Freshy Matcha color system](brand/colorPalette.png)

**Caption:** A palette of vibrant greens and earthy neutrals reflecting purity, balance, and vitality.

The system moves from Matcha Cream and Soft Cream through Light Matcha, Fresh Green, Matcha Leaf, Deep Green, and Forest Shade. Natural Clay and Matcha Stone provide tactile neutrality and high-contrast structure.
